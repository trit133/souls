# Ashen Vigil

A pseudo-3D souls-like roguelite built with Three.js + Vite. A run is three open dungeons, each ending in a mini boss that rewards you with a power-up or a full rest, then the approach to a two-phase final boss. There are no bonfires: death ends the run.

## Number size
Health, damage, healing, flat bonuses and gold are all ×10 what the game was first designed at (`NUM` in `src/scale.js`), so the numbers have room to grow; percentages, speeds, timings, stamina and mana aren't scaled, and status meters fill per point of damage ÷10, so every fight plays exactly as before (same hits to kill, same hits to die, same procs). Older accounts' gold was multiplied ×10 once. Move tables are written at the old size and scaled where a hit is read (`resolveHit`); character, enemy and boss health, tree flat stats, prices and gold are scaled where they're defined. `scripts/scale.mjs` runs the same fights on the build before and after and checks every number is ×10 and everything else equal. **Numbers quoted below are at the old size; multiply health, damage, healing and gold by 10.**

## Run structure
- **Three dungeons, then Varek** (`src/dungeon.js`): The Ashen Warrens (camp, ruins and bridge chambers), The Hollow Deep (village, crypt, ruins), The Drowned Halls (bog, gate, crypt). Each ends in one of the three mini bosses (shuffled per run); after the third comes the Warden's Gate and **Varek** in the Throne of Ash.
- **A dungeon:** 22-25 chambers on a 7 × 7 grid of 28 m cells, joined by corridors: a random tree with a few loops, so there are dead ends, circuits, small junctions and side rooms. Chambers vary in size and shape (wings, bays, round bog chambers, long crypt halls, platforms over the chasm with plank bridges) and are dressed by theme (palisades, tents, pillars, houses, coffins, pools, stone blocks) with the props kept off the paths between doorways, so every chamber can be crossed; a flood fill puts enemies only where you can reach them. About 55-70 enemies (30% / 60% more in a party). You start at a lit bonfire in the middle. Everything comes from the run's seed, so a party all gets the same map. `scripts/dungeon.mjs` builds many seeds and checks everything is reachable; `scripts/dungeon-look.mjs` takes screenshots.
- **The sealed door:** the mini boss's arena is off one edge of the map, somewhere you have to find, behind a door that shows **SEALED 0/3**. Five seal shrines (a glowing crystal and a beam of light you can see over the walls) are spread far apart; break **three** to open the door (`src/seals.js`). Each is guarded:
  - **Elite pack:** an elite and three others (four with two players, five with three). Elites are bigger, glow in their trait's colour, have 2.6× health and hit 25% harder, with one trait: **Frenzied** (faster attacks and movement, shorter pauses), **Armoured** (takes a third less damage, hard to stagger) or **Blazing** (fire on every hit and a heat aura that burns anyone within 2.5 m). Kill the pack and the seal breaks.
  - **Hold the circle:** stand in the ring round the shrine for 30 s. Time only runs with someone inside (it slips back slowly when nobody is) and waves of 3 (4 with two players, 5 with three) come in from the edges every 7 s.
  - **Cursed altar:** three cursed effigies stand round the shrine. Come near and enemies pour out of the altar (2 every 5 s, up to 8; 10 with two players, 12 with three) until all three effigies are broken.
- **Exploring pays:** every enemy you kill is gold, so clearing more of a dungeon earns more; killing every enemy in a dungeon (the map shows % cleared; enemies called in by seals don't count) gives 5 emeralds.
- **The map** (`src/minimap.js`): a corner map that fills in as you and your party explore; **M** (or a tap on it) opens the whole map. Unbroken seals always glow on it, even in unexplored dark; the boss door and arena show once seen. Under it: the dungeon's name, seals broken and % cleared.
- **After the mini boss:** a stair down opens in the arena. Step on it and press interact to go on to the next dungeon (in a party it takes everyone). You keep your health, flasks and power-ups.
- **Enemies in a dungeon** chase you into the chambers next to theirs, but no further. Regular enemies get stronger each dungeon (tier = dungeon number).
- **Night Raid:** unlocks for the whole account once you beat the second mini boss of a run; then a **Night Raid** toggle sits under the character cards (in a party, the host's toggle in the lobby sets it for everyone). The whole run is played at night: near darkness under a faint cold moonlight, each player lights a cone about 5 m ahead (plus a faint glow round them) and teammates' cones light things for you too; torches, braziers and bonfires give only a faint glow (30% as bright, 60% the reach of the day), and enemies outside the light are dark shapes with glowing eyes. The corner map fills in a smaller circle round you. Enemies have +40% health, +30% damage, +20% move speed, 30% shorter pauses between attacks and notice you from 30% further; enemies waiting their turn step in every 5 s instead of every 10-15. Mini bosses and Varek get +40% health and +30% damage. Half of all enemy gold drops are doubled, and boss drops are doubled. `scripts/night.mjs` checks it.
- The old run of 11 rooms in a row still builds for the older test scripts (`?chain` in the address).
- **Chamber themes** (from the old rooms):
  - The Broken Road: winding ruins.
  - The Ashen Camp: palisade war camp.
  - The Hanging Span: narrow bridge over a chasm.
  - The Burned Hamlet: houses to path around, plus a brute.
  - The Ossuary Hall: pillared crypt with blue torches.
  - The Sunken Mire: bog pools that slow you.
  - The Warden's Gate: brute courtyard plus archers past the gate.
- **Mini bosses:**
  - **Brother Hask** (Executioner's Pit): axe; flurry, delayed chop, charge, hop slam.
  - **Sister Vael** (Moonlit Cloister): a moon-masked assassin with a swinging silver braid, a split cloak and two crescent daggers; sidesteps your swings, lunges and dives.
  - **Gorrath** (Slag Forge): giant maul; slams, sweeps, rear stomp, thrown boulders, and a leaping maul drop (a red ring marks the landing) that splashes out a ring of burning slag and swings into a sweep. Still the slowest of the three, but 25% quicker to attack than he was, with shorter pauses and a faster walk. `scripts/gorrath.mjs` checks it.
- **After each mini boss:** pick 1 of 3 random power-ups, or **Rest** (full HP and flasks refilled). Power-ups stack:
  - +25 max HP
  - +15 max stamina
  - +15% damage
  - +15% roll distance
  - +10% attack speed
  - Bleed, Poison, Frost or Fire Infusion: each hit adds 15% of its physical damage as that element (+8% for each extra)
- **Damage types** (`src/damage.js`): physical, fire, bleed, poison and frost. A hit's total comes off the health bar; only elemental damage builds status (bleed damage builds bleed, and so on). Every character's weapon is physical; the skill tree and Infusions add the rest.
- **Status effects** (Elden Ring-style buildup): each point of elemental damage fills that status's meter (×1.75 for your melee, ×0.75 for spells, ×4 for enemy hits on you). When it's full the effect procs and the meter resets. After its first proc on an enemy or a player, that status needs twice the buildup. **On enemies** one hit fills at most 15% of the meter (even a maxed bleed build needs 7 hits on a swordsman, then 14 for the next proc), and each 1% of an enemy's resistance cuts buildup by 3% (at most 90%). `scripts/buildup.mjs` counts the hits.
  - On enemies: bleed is a burst of 15% max HP + 45; frost 10% + 25 and slowed for 6 seconds; poison 20% + 60 over 10 seconds; fire 25% + 50 over 3.5 seconds. Bosses take roughly half the percentage.
  - On players: bleed a burst of 15% max health; fire 15% over 3.5 seconds; poison 15% over 10 seconds; frost 6% and slowed for 4 seconds. Your meters show as big framed icons over your health bar, glowing in their colour with the meter under each; an icon flashes every time a hit builds it and bursts white when it procs.
  - Enemies show small status icons above their health bar while a meter is filling; the icon flashes when it procs.
- **Enemy elements** (teaching you to build resistances): a regular enemy's element is **extra damage on top of its hit** (+20% with one element, +12% each with two; from the third dungeon on +30% / +18% each / +13% each with three). How many carry one rises each dungeon: **half** in the first, **80%** in the second (a third of those with two), **every enemy** in the third and on the way to Varek (45% with two, 10% with three). Elites, seal guards and enemies called in by seals included; Blazing elites always carry fire. Their weapons show it. Bosses' elements are part of their hit instead (the total stays the same): Hask hits with bleed, Vael with frost and bleed (half the buildup per hit: she hits fast), Gorrath with fire, Varek with fire once he ignites. `scripts/elements.mjs` checks the mix.
- **Armour and resistances** (skill tree): the Knight's armour cuts physical damage (up to 60%). Each 1% of an element's resistance cuts that element's damage and its buildup on you by 1%: a direct cut, up to 75% (at the cap a quarter of the damage gets through; at 60% you take 40%, 60% more than capped). The stats panel shows how much of each element you take. The full trees reach the caps; a first-section build holds only part of them.
- **Critical hits** (`critRoll` in `src/defenses.js`): any hit, yours or an enemy's, can crit for +50% damage plus the attacker's crit damage bonus (weapon hits, spells, arrows, Gorrath's rocks and fire, Varek's rings; not ripostes or finishers). Caps: 75% chance, +100% bonus (a crit at most ×2.5). **Player:** every tree has 10 *Precision* nodes (+3% crit chance near the start up to +12% at the edge, 75% in all) and 10 *Ferocity* nodes (+4% up to +16% crit damage, +100% in all), so a first-section build reaches only the inner few. **Every element in every tree:** each class's own elements stay the strongest, and the elements its tree had none of get 4 nodes each, added the same way (Knight: bleed, poison, frost; Rogue: fire, frost; Spellsword: bleed, poison): +20 then +30 flat per hit and +50% then +80% (125% at most with the cap), the bigger ones further out (tree version 15 refunded every tree once). The crit nodes were added on top of the finished trees: each sits in the middle of an existing long line (split in two), or carries a plain branch end one step further, clear of every node and line, one per line and spread round the regions; no other node moved or changed, and every evolution is still 6 links out (tree version 14 refunded every tree once). Your crits show as bigger orange numbers with a "!". **Enemies** roll crit chance and crit damage per run like their defences: archers 2-5% / +20-35%, spearmen and swordsmen 3-6% / +25-40%, brutes 4-8% / +30-45%, elites +3% / +5%; Hask 6-8% / +35-45%, Vael 8-10% / +30-40%, Gorrath 6-8% / +40-50%, Varek 10-12% / +40-50%. Each dungeon adds +1% / +2%, each new section +3% / +6% more (this first section tops out near 15% / +56%). An enemy's crit on you shows "Critical!" over your head with a harder shake. `scripts/crit.mjs` checks it all.
- **Enemy armour and resistances** (`src/defenses.js`): the same rules and caps for enemies (armour 60%, resistances 75%). Every enemy rolls its armour inside its type's range and 1-4 resistances (fire, bleed, poison, frost) inside its type's resistance range, from the run's seed so a party sees the same. At the first dungeon: archers 0-5 armour / 3-8 resistance, spearmen 2-8 / 3-10, swordsmen 5-10 / 5-10, brutes 10-15 / 8-15; elites +5. Bosses: Hask 8-12 / 6-12, Vael 4-8 / 6-12, Gorrath 12-16 / 6-12 with fire always 15-20, Varek 16-20 / 14-20 in all four. Each later dungeon adds +2 armour / +3 resistance; each new section (every 4 dungeons, for the sections to come) adds a further +6 / +9, so this first section tops out around 26 armour / 29 resistance and later ones close in on the caps. Lock on to an enemy and a panel under the minimap shows its damage per hit (its moves averaged, with its scaling and added elements), crit chance and crit damage, armour, resistances and the elements it deals. `scripts/defenses.mjs` checks the ranges, the mitigation and the panel.
- **Weapon effects** (`src/weaponfx.js`): any element you deal at least 1.5 of on a light hit shows on your blade as pixel particles: bleed drips red, fire burns, frost radiates white cold, poison drips green. Two or three blend into a named look (Bloodfire, Crimson Rime, Black Blood, Frostfire, Venom Flame, Rime Venom, Ember Blood Ice, Plague Fire, Frozen Plague, Elemental Venom); all four flash gold. Teammates see yours.
- Module code lives in `src/modules.js`, power-ups in `src/boons.js`, and status effects in `src/status.js`.

## Home screen and the Hermitage
The title has three buttons in the pixel style: a big **Hermitage**, **Settings** and **Fullscreen** (`src/home.js`). Hermitage asks you to sign in first, then opens.

**The Hermitage** (`src/hermitage.js`) is each character's hideout and the hub between runs: a small dark 3D scene drawn through the game's pixel pass, opening on the last character you played (runs come back here when they end). The character sits in the middle by their fire, which is the main light on them; a lantern and some candles pick out the rest. Kept sparse: hideouts will be customizable later.
- **Knight:** a stone-ringed campfire on packed earth with a few flagstones and grass, sitting on a log; an armour stand (wardrobe), an iron-bound chest, a weapon rack and a war banner.
- **Rogue:** a campfire with a cooking spit on a leafy forest floor with moss and roots, sitting on a stump; a post with a cloak, hood and knives (wardrobe), a battered strongbox, a bedroll and crates.
- **Spellsword:** a purple fire in a black stone fireplace on dark flagstones with a faint rune circle, sitting on a stone seat; a tall carved wardrobe, a rune-carved chest, a lectern with a book and candles.

The things in the scene are the buttons, each with a faint outline: hover one and the outline lights up with a label over it (on touch, the first tap labels it, the second opens it). The **character** opens their skill tree, the **wardrobe** their customizer, the **stash** chest opens its lid and the stash panel on the right: 48 slots shared by every character (empty until items arrive). The **jukebox** (behind the fire) opens your records: pick one and it drops into the jukebox's window and spins, and the menus play its song from then on (kept on the account; `src/records.js`). Two to start: *The Ashen Lament* (the title theme) and *Hearthlight* (song `hearth` in `src/music.js`, still to be written: it plays nothing until then); more are to be found later. At the top: back to the title, the character's name with a small **person button** next to it that drops down the character list (the Spellsword shows locked; picking it asks yes/no to unlock it for ◆ 1,000), and your gold. At the bottom: **Solo Raid** and **Party Raid**. Once Night Raid is unlocked, Solo Raid first pops up a Night Raid on/off toggle and Begin; a party sets it in the lobby. `scripts/hermitage.mjs` tests it and takes screenshots.

The other menus (sign in, skins, settings, lobby) share the title's look (`src/pixelmenus.js`): pixel-font headings, buttons as riveted pixel tiles in reds and oranges, dark pixel-framed panels, segmented bars and sliders.

## Gold and permanent upgrades
- **Hearts:** about a fifth of enemies drop a red pixel heart where they fall. Walk over one while hurt and it heals 200 (20 at the old size); at full health it stays put for later. In a party the host rolls the drop and the first player to reach it takes it. A new level clears any left. `scripts/hearts.mjs` tests it.
- **Earning:** gold comes from kills and goes to your account the moment the enemy dies: 15 for a regular enemy, 30 for a brute, 45 for an elite, wherever you are (getting stronger means getting further and killing more). Mini bosses drop 150, 200, then 250, and Varek 300. Night Raid: half of all kills pay double, and bosses double. Tuned so a run buys about one skill tree node (`scripts/income.mjs`).
- **In a party:** every kill pays everyone a share, whoever landed it: 77% each with two players, 62% with three. A party fights 30% / 60% more enemies, so clearing an area leaves each player about what they'd earn alone. Bosses pay everyone their full drop. Dying keeps everything earned so far.
- **Rooms (old chain only):** each room's exit is fogged until every enemy in it is dead. Enemies never notice or chase you across rooms.
- **Fights:** one enemy at a time fights each player. Others who have noticed you hover nearby with their weapon up, drifting in and out, and step in for one attack every 10-15s. Hitting one of them makes it your fight.
- **Enemy tiers:** each mini boss passed gives everything after it +10% health, damage, move speed and attack speed (additive). In a party, regular enemies instead get +20% / +40% / +60% health and +15% / +20% / +25% damage per stage (speeds as solo).
- **Boss stun:** a boss knocked to its knees by stun needs twice as much for every knockdown after the first. Eyes turn red, then orange, then white with a red halo.
- **Skill trees** (title screen, lobby and after a run; `src/skilltree.js`, screen in `src/treeui.js`): each character has its own web of 215 nodes over 22 rings around a start node: the Knight's a cross with octagon rings and wheels in the gaps, the Rogue's a spiral of long and short arms with sweeping arcs, the Spellsword's a five-point star with gem wheels and forks. Every tree has about 10 nodes a ring: 80 in rings 1-8, 70 in rings 9-15 and 65 in rings 16-22, so each costs about the same to complete (~31,000 gold). Nodes never overlap and link lines never cross (`scripts/gen-trees.mjs` saves the positions to `src/treelayout.js`; run it after changing a shape). Hover a node to see what it gives; **click once to unlock** it (it must touch a node you own). Prices: rings 1-8 10 -> 80, rings 9-15 100 -> 200, rings 16-22 250 -> 350; evolution 150; rune sockets 1.5x their ring.
- **The Knight's tree (regions):** his cross is four regions, one down each arm: **Bulwark** (up: block, armour, guard counter, Shield Charge; the evolution sits here), **Executioner** (right: physical damage, stun, heavy blows, combo finishers), **Crusader** (down: fire, charge, speed), **Warden** (left: health, stamina, potions, resistances). The centre (rings 1-4) holds the basics on every tree: health, stamina (and mana for the Spellsword), a little utility and a little of the class's own damage; no resistances. Small nodes cycle through their quarter's own stats (no filler), worth more the further out they sit: ×1 in the centre, ×1.25 (rings 5-8), ×1.75 (9-13), ×2.5 (14-18), ×3 at the edge (19-22). Many stats reach their cap well before a full tree. Six **notables** per region sit at branch ends (gold rings, twice a small node's price): bundles like *Iron Wall* (−15% block drain, −3 chip) or mechanics: *Riposte Master* (guard counters give back 15 stamina), *Executioner's Mark* (+25% damage to enemies under 30%), *Last Stand* (+25% under half health), *Crowning Blow* (+25% finisher damage), *Pyre* (+20% to burning enemies), *Second Wind* (heal 3% per kill). A **keystone** caps each arm (three times the price): *Immovable* (never staggered or knocked down; rolls cost 50% more), *Headsman* (heavies and finishers +40%, light attacks −20%), *Ember Oath* (half your physical damage becomes fire; you take 15% more), *Stalwart Vow* (+30% health, −15% damage). Four **evolutions**, one per region: *Shield Charge* (Bulwark) works; *Guillotine* (Executioner), *Sun Brand* (Crusader) and *Rallying Cry* (Warden) evolve abilities still to come, so they show with their notes (purple, dashed) and can't be taken yet. The region names sit faintly under the nodes, the same distance out along each arm. Moving to it refunded every Knight node once at the price paid (tree version 8; version 9 refunded anything bought where the new evolutions now sit); the Rogue's and Spellsword's trees are unchanged for now. `scripts/knight-tree.mjs` tests it.
- **Evolution spacing:** like the first evolution, the three still to come sit exactly 6 links from the start (one on each other arm where the tree has them), at least 6 links from each other, on nodes whose loss cuts nothing off. The Rogue's six close-set arms leave no such spot for Crimson Fan nearer than 8. They are placed after every other node's stats are set, so moving one changes nothing else. (Tree versions 11 and 12 refunded every tree once for the moves.)
- **The Rogue's tree (regions):** **Shadow** (up: rolls, i-frames, parry cooldown; Red Riposte), **Bloodletter** (right: bleed, ripostes), **Venom** (down: poison), **Duelist** (left: attack speed, stamina, combos). The parry window grows only a little: two small +2 ms nodes in Shadow, capped at +4 ms. Notables include *Ghost Step*, *Quick Hands*, *Exsanguinate* (+20% damage to enemies whose bleed is half built), *Cruel Edge*, *Wither* (+20% to poisoned enemies), *Virulence*, *Flurry*, *Finishing Flourish*. Keystones: *Fleeting* (rolls cost half; you take 15% more), *Glass Fang* (ripostes and finishers +40%, −25% health), *Plaguelord* (poison +50%, physical −20%), *Blade Dance* (+25% attack speed, −20% per hit). Evolutions to come: *Crimson Fan*, *Plague Cloud*, *Blade Waltz*.
- **The Spellsword's tree (regions):** **Pyromancer** (up: fire, fireball; Twin Inferno), **Frostblade** (right: frost, knife), **Arcanist** (down: mana, spells), **Spellward** (left: health, resistances, potions). Notables include *Inferno*, *Wide Blast*, *Pyre*, *Shatterpoint* (+20% to enemies slowed by frost), *Cold Steel*, *Deep Well*, *Siphon*, *Arcane Power*, *Wardstone*. Keystones: *Pyromania* (fire +40%; you take 15% more from fire), *Rime Heart* (frost +40%, fire halved), *Spellblade* (spells +30%, knife −20%), *Stalwart Vow*. Evolutions to come: *Glacier Prison*, *Arcane Echo*, *Runic Aegis*. Moving the Rogue and Spellsword to regions refunded their nodes once at the price paid (tree version 10). `scripts/trees.mjs` tests both.
  - **Node limit per tree**, from that character's own progress: 45 in the first section, 70 once that character has beaten Varek, 100 in the second section and 130 after beating it (the second section is still to come).
  - **Backgrounds** (`src/treebg.js`): a pixel-art scene per character, painted in code at about the game's resolution, with the middle kept dark for the web (no painted rings: the tree draws its own). The scene drifts a tenth of the way with the tree when you drag it, and scales slightly with the zoom. Knight: the Hall of Fallen Kings (two knight statues with planted swords, torn crimson banners, broken stained glass with moonbeams, gold braziers, a royal floor circle, rising embers). Braziers, black flames, spirit flames, the rune circle and moonbeams are pixelfx effects.
  - **Search** (top right of the tree screen): type words and every node whose name or description has them all lights up with a pulsing gold ring while the rest dim, with a count ("crit chance", "fire resistance", "notable", a region's full name). Esc or the × clears it; it carries over when you switch characters. `scripts/treesearch.mjs` tests it.
  - **Stats panel** on the left of the tree screen: that character's health, stamina, flasks, speeds, damage per type with a light hit worked out, armour and resistances, and their own bonuses. Hovering a node you don't own shows what it would change (in green).
  - Inner rings: flat health and stamina. Middle rings: more of those and early +1% health or stamina. Outer rings: mostly percentages (health, stamina, move or attack speed, potion healing), some bigger flat chunks, and two extra flasks.
  - **Damage nodes** for every type in every tree, leaning to the character's own. Knight: the most physical, +15 flat over ten nodes and +5/8/10/12/15% (50%). Rogue bleed and Spellsword frost: +7 flat and +15/20/25/30/35% (125%), plus +6 physical flat and +5/8% physical. Every other element: +1 and +2 flat and +15/20/25% (60%). Small values near the centre, the biggest at the edge.
  - **Resistances** in every tree: fire, bleed, poison and frost, none inside ring 5: 7 nodes per element (7, 8, 9, 10, 12, 13, 16%: 75%) rising outward, five of each seven in the defensive quarter (Warden, Venom, Spellward). **Armour** (Knight only, cap 60%): 2% nodes round the centre (two in Bulwark, one in each other quarter), a 3% node in the middle of each other quarter, and Bulwark's own 3% to 5% going out (60% in all, plus Unbroken and Bastion). Regrouping the stats refunded every tree once (tree version 16).
  - Character nodes, small and large (capped so a full tree stays fair): Knight block stamina drain, shield chip, stun buildup, guard counter damage, charge cooldown and knockback; Rogue parry cooldown, riposte and finisher damage, roll invincibility, attack and roll stamina cost, parry window (+1 / +3 ms, max 10 ms); Spellsword max mana, mana per hit, bolt and fireball cost, spell damage, blast radius, knife damage.
  - Evolution: 6 rings out. Three empty rune sockets sit on ring 10 (dashed violet), a third of the way round from each other and joined by the same 3-node chain on every tree, so going socket to socket costs the same for every character. Runes come later.
  - **Reset**: click owned nodes to mark them (anything they would cut off is marked too), then Confirm to get their full price back.
  - Hover shows a node's details and they clear as soon as you move off; on touch a tap buys and shows it until the next tap.
  - The old shop's spending, and anything bought on earlier tree layouts (including the 180-node one), is refunded once at the price paid; the Spellsword unlock (100) stays. `scripts/tree.mjs` tests it; `scripts/treecheck.mjs` checks the layout (no overlaps).

- **Accounts:** online, in `server/` (Node + SQLite). Passwords are hashed with scrypt, and each device gets its own login token. Rules live in `src/progress.js`.

## Multiplayer (2-3 players)
Create a room, share the 4-letter code, and the host starts the run. The host's browser runs the enemies; each player runs their own character, so dodges are judged on that player's device. Code is in `src/mp.js`.

| | 2 players | 3 players |
|---|---|---|
| Bosses and brutes: health | +75% | +200% |
| Bosses and brutes: damage / attack speed | +20% / +10%, 30% shorter gaps | same |
| Regular enemies: health | +10% | +13% |
| Regular enemies: damage | +10% | +12% |
| All enemies: move speed | +10% | +10% |
| Extra regular enemies | +30% | +60% |

- **Gold:** every signed-in player banks the full room and boss amounts on their own account, including section bonuses.
- **Going down:** you stay down until a teammate revives you (50% health). Each revive takes 10% of max health from everyone for the rest of the run. If everyone is down, the run ends.
- **Rewards:** each player picks their own, and the run resumes when everyone standing has chosen.
- **Teammates:** their attacks play out on your screen from instant events, and their sounds play at half volume. Every ability effect (ground pound, knee impact, shroud, red slash, rune impale, cast glow and circle, explosions, heals, shield bashes, status bursts, Varek's Ember Burst and ignite) is a named effect in `src/vfx.js`: the device that performs it plays it and sends it, and every other screen plays the same thing. Jumps, the shroud vanish, reverse grips and the shield-charge shield show on teammates too. `scripts/mpfx.mjs` checks it with two devices.
- **After a run:** everyone taps Ready Up, and the host's device rolls a new run in the same room. **Lobby & Upgrades** goes back to the room's lobby to shop and change class; the host can start from there.
- **Down when a mini boss dies:** you get back up at half health (no revive penalty) and pick a reward like everyone else.
- If the host leaves, the run ends for everyone.

## Server
Runs as the user service `souls-server` on port 4100, public at `/souls` through Tailscale Funnel. Data is in `server/data/` (gitignored). The unit file is in `deploy/`. To reinstall: `cd server && npm i`.

**Pixel-art look** (`src/pixel.js`): the 3D scene renders at about 270 game pixels tall, gets 1-pixel outlines, hard 3-step lighting and a fixed palette with ordered dithering, and is scaled up by a whole number with CSS `image-rendering: pixelated`. This is also cheap on big and ultrawide screens. Screens wider than 2.4:1 get a 2.4:1 horizontal view. `scripts/look.mjs <tag>` takes review screenshots.

**Living models** (`src/models.js`, `src/kits.js`): every character is built from boxes with per-character detail. Cloth, capes, plumes, scarves and hair hang on springs that react to movement, turns and a gusting wind; armor plates rattle on footsteps and hits (the Knight's also clink); arms, torso and head get a little follow-through. The Rogue is a hooded, cloaked figure with curved daggers and an idle dagger flip; the Spellsword's coat runes glow with her mana and glyphs orbit her off hand; Varek's armor cracks glow once he ignites. All visual: hit-boxes are circles around each actor.

**Set dressing and ambient life** (`src/decor.js`): every room gets props along its walls and on its rim (corpses, bones, gravestones, skull stakes, carts, sacks, candles, lantern posts, swords stuck in the ground, blood), figures (kneeling pilgrims, chained prisoners, weeping statues) and small life that only moves near you (crows that take off as you come close, rats along the walls, swaying hanging cages, moths, fireflies, falling ash, dust). The arenas are themed: the Executioner's Pit has a hooded crowd swaying round the rim, spikes, gallows cages and blood-soaked sand; the Moonlit Cloister broken arches, moonbeams, a glowing pool, candles and praying statues with drifting petals; the Slag Forge lava channels, cauldrons pouring slag, smoking chimneys and anvils; the Throne of Ash a ruined throne, torn standards, cracked flagstones, swords in the ground and braziers that flare when Varek ignites. Props never sit in the walkable area (the few inside the walls have no collision) and are lifted onto the stepped terrain (`World.groundY`), so layouts and fights are unchanged. `scripts/rooms-look.mjs` takes screenshots of every room.

**Combat effects** (`src/pfx.js`): pixelfx effects drawn into small canvases on camera-facing sprites, on the same pixel grid as the scene: hit sparks, crits, parries, blocks, heals, death poofs, the Spellsword's charge-up, magic circle, blue bolt and churning fireball with explosion, and Varek's Ember Burst. Effects copied from the pixelfx library live in `src/pixelfx/fx/`. `scripts/models.mjs` and `scripts/effects.mjs` take review screenshots.

It runs in desktop browsers and on iPhone (landscape, touch controls). You can add it to the Home Screen for fullscreen.

## Characters
- **Knight**: longsword and shield. 150 HP, 3-hit combo plus heavy. Hold **L1** to block: it drains 10 stamina a second and a quarter of each blocked hit still gets through. Press **heavy** right after blocking a hit for a guard counter, a quick forehand-backhand (21 + 21). The Knight builds the most stun by far (light 15, third swing 25, heavy 37).
- **Rogue**: twin daggers. 105 HP, fast 4-hit combo, lunge heavy, longer i-frames. **L1** parries (tight window, 8 s cooldown from any attempt, 5 s with Red Riposte, 1.5 s less after a successful parry; the HUD shows it). A successful parry opens a riposte. Builds little stun.

## Combat notes
- **Combos** (`src/combos.js`): start with a normal attack; at a set moment in each move the weapon glints and a little red mark shows over your head, and pressing the next input within **270 ms** continues the combo (a chime plays). Pressing outside the window does that button's normal move; doing nothing ends it. Combos branch from shared openings. Each class has three 3-move combos and one 4-move combo whose moves play 15% faster (the four evolution combos to come split the same way). Every attack costs 10% less stamina than its listed cost; combo moves cost a fixed, larger amount. The last move is always the combo's biggest hit. A hit during a combo's first move breaks it; from the second move on you are armoured: hits still hurt, but they don't stagger, knock down or shove you, and the combo carries on. A combo staggers each enemy at most once, so nothing gets stun-locked. Combo moves turn to an enemy right behind you (after the roll steps). The skill tree screen lists them under the stats. `scripts/combos.mjs` plays each one on time, early and late.
- **Combo holds** (`src/hold.js`): a combo hit from the second move on that lands while an enemy or boss is *weak* holds it in a light stagger (its attack is cut off; no knockdown). Each combo hit keeps the hold going for 0.9 s, so it lasts until the combo ends. Weak means idle, walking, chasing, recovering, staggered, mid parryable attack, or recovering after an unparryable attack's last hit. Never weak: on its knees for a finisher, parried, being riposted, dormant, rising, Varek's roar, Vael's sidestep. Held time adds up across players (gaps under a second don't reset it; 2 s fully free does). After 4 s a regular enemy shrugs free and can't be held for 3 s; a boss or brute breaks out with a burst of energy (5 m ring, pushes everyone back, 8% of max health, rolls dodge it) and can't be held for 8 s. In multiplayer the host applies holds; teammates' combo hits are flagged. `scripts/hold.mjs` checks it.

- **Stamina:** at 0 you can't attack, roll, backstep or parry (combos stop too) until it refills a little. Long attack strings slow walking by at most 10%.
- Inputs are buffered (0.4s). Combos chain inside a window. Rolls can cancel attack recovery after a set point.
- Hitstop, camera shake, weapon trails, and poise/stagger on both sides.
- **Damage numbers** pop out of enemies and fade, one per damage type in the hit: physical white, fire orange, bleed red, poison green, frost icy blue. Heavy hits are bigger, ripostes and finishers gold, critical hits orange with a "!"; teammates' are dimmer. Status damage shows in its colour (damage over time is added up and shown in batches).
- One enemy fights each player at a time; the others watch and strike every 10-15s. Enemy types: grunts (slash, double, and a long dash-and-slam leap from up to 4.8 m), spearmen (long thrusts, sweep when you are close), archers (keep distance, arrows can be sidestepped), and a super-armored brute (telegraphed slam and a stomp when you stand behind it).
- **Varek, the Ashen Warden** has 1300 HP.
  - Phase 1: sweeps, a 2-hit combo, a *delayed* overhead, a lunge thrust, a leap slam with a ground telegraph, and a spin if you hug his back.
  - At 55% HP he roars and his blade ignites. He gets faster and adds a 3-hit combo, fire waves, a fire ring on his leap, and Ember Burst (a telegraphed AoE).
  - Parry or poise-break him for a critical. He punishes healing at mid range.
- Red glints and red ground circles mark unparryable attacks.
- **Parry (Rogue):**
  - Timing: press it within the 0.23s before a parryable hit lands. A bright, pulsing white ring (with a dark edge so it shows on any ground) on the attacker shows exactly that window.
  - What can be parried: only certain light attacks (grunt slashes, spear thrust and sweep, archer bash, Varek's sweeps, Hask's chop and flurry). Projectiles, slams, overheads, leaps, charges, lunges, spins and stomps never can.

- **Ripostes and finishers** (a riposte on a parried enemy; a finisher on a boss knocked to its knees by a poise break): each character has its own. (These used to be called critical hits; *critical hits* are now the random crits below.) The tree's *Cruelty* nodes add riposte and finisher damage.
  - Knight, Flying Knee: 110. Leaps high and drives a knee into the enemy's head; blood sprays off it.
  - Rogue after a parry, Parry Stab: 48 (three light hits plus 3× the tree's flat damage of every type, times damage bonuses; ×2.3 on bosses, 110). Evolved: blinks behind the enemy in a red flash and stabs it in the back.

## Music
All music is synthesized in `src/music.js`: songs are written as 16th-note patterns and played by a 16-bit style band (pulse-wave leads, triangle bass, noise drums) over an orchestral layer (strings, choir, brass stabs, timpani, a bell) in a cathedral reverb.
- **Title (all the menus):** Varek's motif slowed to a lament at 76 BPM: a harp arpeggio, choir and bells, with a warmer major middle. It starts on the first tap or key (browsers hold sound until then), fades out when a run starts and comes back on the title, lobby or skill trees after a run.
- **Jukebox records** (song ids for `src/records.js`): `title` The Ashen Lament; `hearth` Hearthlight (G major by the fire: a lute over strings with the logs crackling, 84 BPM); `ember` Ember Waltz (a C major music box in 3/4); `pilgrim` Pilgrim's Road (a hopeful D major walking tune, lute strummed on the off beats); `moonwell` Moonwell (still and dreamy, E minor: bells, a music box and the choir at 60 BPM).
- **Exploring** (`src/ambience.js` for the world's sounds, under the Effects volume): quiet pads (`drift`, A minor, 64 BPM) under gusting wind, with sounds drifting in from either side at different distances: creaking wood, wind whistling through stone and crows in the Ashen Warrens; drips, embers and wind whistling through stone in the Hollow Deep; lapping water, frogs and insects in the Drowned Halls. **Night Raid** plays `dread` instead (a D drone, the half step above pressing in, a slow heartbeat, a low chant and a far bell) over a howling wind, with far screams, moans, whispers close by, a distant bell, dragged chains and growls. Boss fights fade the music to the boss's theme and drop the world's sounds back; they come back when the boss falls. Death, victory and the menus stop them.
- **Varek, Throne of Ash:** D minor, 132 BPM with a bell and choir intro. When he ignites in his second phase it switches on the next bar line into a faster 148 BPM version with a new melody.
- **Brother Hask, the Executioner's Pit:** an E Phrygian march at 112 BPM: war drums, a low "oh" chant and a blade clang on every fourth beat, with a faster flurry section.
- **Sister Vael, the Moonlit Cloister:** a quick A minor waltz in 3/4 at 152 BPM, plucked strings on the off beats and a break of cloister bells.
- **Gorrath, the Slag Forge:** G minor at 156 BPM, a chugging bass, hammer-on-anvil hits on the off beats and a half-time section for the maul.
- `jukebox.html` (dev server only: `npx vite --host`, then open `/jukebox.html`) plays each song. `scripts/music.mjs` renders them offline, reports peak and loudness for the whole mix and each instrument, and writes WAVs to `/tmp`.
