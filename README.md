# Ashen Vigil

> **Retheme in progress: THE LAST CRUSADE** (grimdark dark science fantasy). Visuals and names only; gameplay is untouched and internal ids stay the same (saves, accounts and skill trees carry over). Display names: Knight → **Templar**, Rogue → **Shrike**, Spellsword → **Adept**; Hermitage → **Sanctum**; flasks → **injectors**; seals → **ward shrines** (the circle is an **uplink**, the altar's effigies **heretic idols**); bosses **Executor Hask, the Iron Penitent**, **Sister Vael, Blade of the Cryo Choir**, **Gorrath, the Slag Engine**, **Archon Varek, the Last Sentinel**; dungeons **The Rust Warrens**, **The Hollow Forge-Deep**, **The Coolant Drowns**. Elements keep their names; music unchanged for now. Enemies on the lock-on panel: Zealot, Pike Zealot, Needle Gunner, War Servitor, Heretic Idol. **World (done):** ferrocrete and deck-plate floors with seams, iron for wood, girder wrecks and gothic spire-pylons with warning lamps on the skyline, ferrocrete columns with iron bands, munitions crates, promethium drums, crusade standards, steel barricades, sodium work-lamps (no flame), field heaters, hab-bunkers with lit window slits, coolant spills, crimson energy barriers for the fog gates, and the bonfire is a **reliquary beacon** that ignites blue-white plasma round a sacred blade. Set dressing: cultists with red optics, servo-skulls instead of crows, scuttle-drones instead of rats, frost motes in the cryo cloister. Every prop keeps its old calls to the level random stream and its collision, so layouts are identical (`scripts/layout-fingerprint.mjs` hashes them; `scripts/look-world.mjs` takes screenshots). **Hideouts (done):** the Templar's chapel-bunker (a promethium fire-bowl in a vented steel hearth, a crusade standard, a powered weapon rack, a shrine with a servo-skull, a cogitator, an armour cradle, an armoured reliquary chest), the Shrike's gutted hab-room over the burning hive (fire in a cut-down fuel drum, bedroll and respirator, ammo crates, a tarp, a smashed window on the red skyline, a trophy servo-skull) and the Adept's psy-sanctum (a reactor-hearth with a blue plasma flame behind copper coils, a circuit-sigil warding circle, capacitor pylons, a cogitator-lectern with a data-slate, a stasis casket, a robe in a stasis tube); the jukebox is a vox-caster. Same places, fires, lights and buttons. **Enemies and bosses (done):** Zealots with chain-blades, rebreathers or brass-ringed helms, gunmetal vests, purity seals and torn crusade-red tabards; Pike Zealots with shock-pikes (blue-arcing prongs, a power coil), red cowls, a vox-unit and whip antenna; Needle Gunners with a needle rifle, goggles and a glowing power-pack; War Servitors in plate with exhaust stacks, a grilled face, hoses and a piston hammer; Heretic Idols with spiked halos and burning runes. Executor Hask wears a riveted iron penitent mask with a burning slit and swings a chain-axe; Sister Vael carries coolant cylinders; Gorrath is a slag engine with molten seams, a furnace grate and exhaust stacks, swinging a piston maul with a slag core; Archon Varek has a spiked iron halo, a back reactor, gold trim, purity seals and a relic blade. Same rigs, sizes and weapon lengths, so every attack plays as before (`scripts/look-models.mjs` takes close-ups). **Player characters (done):** on every outfit, the Templar gets a resonance core glowing down his blade, a reactor pack with exhaust stacks and cables, a glowing visor slit, a purity seal and an aegis emitter on the shield, in gunmetal, crusade red and brass by default; the Shrike gets red augmetic optics, a cloak-field generator with cables to the wrists and red mono-edges on the knives; the Adept gets a turning halo emitter, neural ports and a spine cable. **Effects (done):** the Adept's fireball is a crimson plasma charge (pixelfx plasma-shot) with a plasma burst; parries discharge electric blue; the heal is a bio-green regeneration surge from the injector; the Templar's Aegis Ram flares an electric energy dome (pixelfx shield-block) and his charge barrier glows blue; spell charges and circles are electric. `scripts/look-fx.mjs` takes screenshots. Every stage is done; the music is still the old one for now. **Second pass:** every one of the 70 outfit pieces has its own crusade layer and a new name (`src/crusadekit.js`: e.g. the Crusader Helm's vox grille and cabled collar, the Sanctioned Sallet's rebreather hoses and antenna, the Breacher Plate's hazard chevrons, Servo-Greaves' knee pistons, Mag-Boots, the Chain-Falchion's teeth, the Aegis Shield's cog, the Shrike's Rebreather Wrap, Vox Half-Mask, Death's-Head optic, Data Bracer screen, Chain Fangs, the Adept's Coil Pauldron, Magos Robe cables, Circuit Leggings, Plasma Torch); ids unchanged, so owned pieces carry over (`scripts/look-outfits.mjs` shoots every set). The Shrike's hide is red; the Templar's shrine no longer hides the chest. Enemies vary one to the next (Zealots with crusade icons on poles, bandoliers, pauldrons, red hoods; Pike Zealots with rebreathers, breastplates, seals; Needle Gunners with antennae and range-finders; War Servitors with shoulder skulls and furnace packs), and every room's edge has structures by its theme: gothic buttresses with lit slits, pipe runs with valves, hanging cable bundles, statues of armoured saints with haloes, wrecked servitors, gun emplacements, cogitator stacks and chained reliquaries (placed by a position hash, no collision, layouts unchanged).

A pseudo-3D souls-like built with Three.js + Vite. You pick a raid off a world map: regions, each with three hand-built raid areas. Find and break three shrines, find the boss's hidden lair, kill the boss. Victory or death are the only ways out.

> **Lore:** the old regime looked into the future with sorcery and stole its machines for their arms and armour. It only fed the old wars until everything burned. The player characters are the last of that regime, fighting their way toward the one who started it all.

## Number size
Health, damage, healing, flat bonuses and gold are all ×10 what the game was first designed at (`NUM` in `src/scale.js`), so the numbers have room to grow; percentages, speeds, timings, stamina and mana aren't scaled, and status meters fill per point of damage ÷10, so every fight plays exactly as before (same hits to kill, same hits to die, same procs). Older accounts' gold was multiplied ×10 once. Move tables are written at the old size and scaled where a hit is read (`resolveHit`); character, enemy and boss health, tree flat stats, prices and gold are scaled where they're defined. `scripts/scale.mjs` runs the same fights on the build before and after and checks every number is ×10 and everything else equal. **Numbers quoted below are at the old size; multiply health, damage, healing and gold by 10.**

## Raids
- **How to Play** (`src/guide.js`): a tab at the top left of the Sanctum, next to Title, opens a framed guide with a page per topic (The Goal, A Raid, Shrines & Boss, Loot & Escape, Fighting, Controls with your own key bindings, Getting Stronger, Playing Together). It opens by itself the first time an account enters the Sanctum.
- **The world map** (`src/worldmap.js`, data in `src/regions.js`): **Solo Raid** in the Sanctum opens it. Five regions on one ashen continent (only **The Shattered March** is open so far); click a region to see its own map and its three raid areas, pick one to read about it, and **Deploy**. Night Raid is toggled there once unlocked. A party raid goes to the first area for now. After a raid, **Back to Map**.
- **Ashfall Ward** (`src/city.js`), the first area: a burned-out hab-ward about 100 m across, the same map every raid, laid out as a maze. Lanes 4 m wide run between a 7 × 7 grid of junctions as a winding spanning tree plus two loops; every lane that wasn't carved is a broken brick wall banked with rubble (23 of them), so lanes turn, branch and dead-end, and eight rubble-and-scrap chokepoints narrow lanes to a 2 m gap (never in front of a doorway). Between the lanes, 12 m blocks: houses you enter through their doorways (some split by an inner wall), roofless ruins, walled yards, rubble heaps, four small squares, and a chapel, a manufactorum and a barracks each over two blocks. You start in the crusade's camp south of the gate.
- **Inside and out:** a house's roof and walls drop to a metre only once you're **inside** it; from outside it stays whole. When a building stands between you and the camera it fades to glass, its inside filled with dark so you still can't see in (and anyone in there stays hidden); behind a lane wall your outline shows through it (enemies too, in red, within 20 m). Roofed houses are **dark** inside (no lamps; ruins and yards stay lit): the daylight goes and your **vision cone** comes on, as in Night Raid. The same happens underground. Six buildings have **stairs down** (F) to vaults and bunkers, built far to the east and joined by the stairs.
- **Walls stop everything:** arrows, bolts, fireballs and boulders end at a wall (checked along their path each frame, so a fast one can't skip a thin wall); fireball bursts and every melee swing (yours and theirs) need a clear line through to the target; archers only shoot when they can see you, otherwise they come round.
- **What changes each raid** (from the run's seed, so a party shares it): which three of the squares and vaults hold the **shrines** (at least one on a square and one down in a vault; one of each kind: elite pack, hold the uplink, heretic idols, as in the dungeons), which big building hides the stair to the **boss's vault**, and where all ~55-65 enemies stand. Shrines show on the map only once you've seen them (on the squares they throw a beam of light over the roofs). In the vault a corridor leads to the sealed door (**LOCKED 0/3**); three broken shrines open it, and Executor Hask waits in the pit past it. His death ends the raid.
- **Supply chests** (`src/chests.js`, placed by the map: `src/city.js`): 2-4 a raid, each inside a building (where changes every raid) with two more enemies than usual round it. A chest stays sealed (red lock) until those guards are dead, then F opens it (gold lock and glow). Solo: gold 2-3x what its guards were worth, 1-10 emeralds and one more injector for the raid (none if you're already at your maximum). In a party one opening pays everyone: the gold cut like a kill's (`partyShare`), 1-5 emeralds each, and only the first chest opened gives injectors; every chest after that heals everyone 30% of their max health. The host decides what's inside (a guest's F asks the host). `scripts/chests.mjs` tests it solo and with two players.
- **Basements only where something waits** (`src/city.js`): six buildings can have a stair down, but each raid only opens the ones it needs: the boss's vault, the vault of every shrine that's underground this raid (1 or 2), and one **reliquary hoard**, so 3-4 a raid and never an empty one; the other buildings have no stair. The hoard's pillared hall holds a large chest with a small one either side, guarded by a War Servitor and two big elites (Zealots or Pike Zealots at a War Servitor's size, health, poise and super armour, plus the elite trait). All three chests stay sealed until all three guards are dead. The small ones are ordinary chests; the large one pays 4-6x the guards' gold, 10-20 emeralds (5-10 each in a party) and always an injector (on top of a party's first-chest one). **Marks:** once anyone goes down a stair, an icon shows on that building's street side (drawn over the walls) and on the map, for the whole party (`src/basements.js`): a shrine in its colour (grey and crossed once broken), the hoard's chest (open once all three are looted), or the boss's skull. `scripts/basements.mjs` tests it solo and with two players.
- **Enemies** wear the area's own colours (scavenger gangs in rag and scrap plate), find their way round walls and through doorways to you (a flow field on a 1 m grid, used only when a wall is in the way), and keep to their 25 m chunk of the ward and the ones round it, or to their basement. The dead stay dead.
- **The map** shows walls as lines and each basement on its own (the one you're standing in). `scripts/raid.mjs` checks it all end to end: the same map, a new draw, everything reachable, the stairs, an enemy pathing in through a doorway, a dark house and the cone, a building fading to glass in front of you, an archer that can't shoot through a wall, the dead staying dead, the boss door, the way out in the pit and the end of the raid, and the world map.
- **Materials** (`src/items.js`, tables per area in `src/regions.js`): each region has 10, common to legendary, each with a gold value (common 5, uncommon 15, rare 50, epic 150, legendary 500; region 2 ×2, region 3 ×4; shown only, for a bazaar later). The Shattered March: Scrap Iron, Ash Rag, Spent Casings · Copper Coil, Promethium Residue, Cracked Lens · Servo Motor, Purity Seal · Augury Shard · Oracle Core. Each has a hand-drawn 16×16 icon. **Supply drums** (rusted drums with hazard bands and an amber light, ~15 a raid in houses, yards and lanes) smash with any swing in its striking moment (or a spell) and burst into staves, hoops and a lid that bounce and settle, for a material or two off the area's drum table; the plain dark drums in the manufactorum and the cellars break the same way but are empty half the time. What you get shows in big lines with its icon, in its rarity's colour (epic and legendary glow). **The pouch** (I, or the POUCH button under your bars) lists what you carry in the raid, rarest first, with counts and worth, and how much you'd keep if you fell. **Chests** each roll a rarity (their glow shows it): a common chest has one guard more than its house would, each rarity up one more, rare and up bring an elite, epic two or three, legendary a War Servitor too; inside, its top item at that rarity and two or three a step or two lower. The hoard's large chest rolls the area's hoard table (rare to legendary) and holds two top items and four or five more. In a party everyone gets the same, about half as much each.
- **Keeping it:** what you pick up rides in your pouch (the map's line shows how much and how safe). Leave through a **way out** and you keep it all: the camp gate where you came in, a hatch hidden at the end of a lane on the north, west or east edge (somewhere new each raid), and, once the boss is dead, a green-lit way out in the pit (in a party a door takes everyone). Die (or quit) and you keep a third of it for each shrine broken, the rarest first; all three shrines keep everything. Gold is always kept.
- **Gems:** each mini boss drops a gem in the shape of its head (Penitent's Gem, Cryo Choir Gem, Slag Engine Gem), straight to your account; you only need one, so a repeat kill pays 250 gold more instead. A region's gems will be needed (not used up) to pass the gatekeeper to its main boss.
- **The stash** (the Reliquary in the Sanctum) has three tabs: **Items** (materials, rarest first, with counts; hover or tap for what it is, its region and what it's worth, and the stash's total worth), **Gems** (every boss's, dark until you have it) and **Runes** (later). `scripts/loot.mjs` checks it all with a real account.
- **Weapons** (`src/weapons.js`, moves in `src/weaponmoves.js`): each class starts with one, and each region adds one more per class, forged at the **workbench** in the Sanctum from that region's materials (lots of commons, some uncommons, and its rarest, which only the harder areas drop: Ashfall Ward tops out at rare, so the Shattered March's weapons wait for the second area). A new weapon isn't stronger, just different: its own light chain, heavy, three combos with their own animations, a walking stance, a model with four wardrobe skins, and its own special on the block / parry / plasma button. Each weapon has **one evolution**, built at the workbench and working only with that weapon; the starting weapons' (Aegis Ram, Red Riposte, Twin Plasma) cost Ashfall Ward's commons, uncommons and rares. Evolutions left the skill tree: their nodes are notables now (Weapon Mastery and three more), and whoever owned one gets that evolution built (tree version 19). The **equipment** stand (front right in each hideout) equips a weapon you own and switches its evolution on or off; the wardrobe shows the equipped weapon's skin slot. The crafting menu shows weapons not yet made as black silhouettes with their recipe (have / need), and the regions still to come as dark boxes. The account joeandre has every weapon.
  - **Penitent Slab** (Templar): a Guts-sized slab of iron carried over the shoulder. Two body-turning sweeps and an overhead that cracks the ground; the heavy is a leaping slam. Combos: Dragon's Wheel (two full turns), Anvil (slam, rising cut, buried overhead that floors everything near it), Struggler (sweep, roll, rising cut, a thrust through). **Blade Guard**: the flat of the blade as a wall: blows barely move you, but you hardly walk and each costs more stamina. Evolution **Beast Within**: guard + heavy for 8 s of no stagger and 25% faster swings, taking 20% more (25 s cooldown).
  - **Wrist Blade** (Shrike): a blade from the right bracer: punching stabs and an uppercut; the heavy is a short jab. Combos: Silent Count (two stabs, one up under the jaw), Shadow Hook (stab, roll, a long lunge), Needle Rain (jab, two stabs, a leaping downward drive). **Blink Dash**: a 3.6 m dash in a fifth of a second, untouchable all the way, ready again after 3 s. Evolution **Ghost Veil**: after a dash you fade for 5 s into a pale shade of your armour's colour trailing ghosts; enemies lose you, and your first strike into the back of one facing away does triple damage. Attacking or being struck ends it.
  - **Psy-Staff** (Adept): a long staff held in both hands: reaching sweeps and a thrust that charge mana; the heavy is Arc Lash, a cone of lightning (10 mana). Combos: Rising Staff (two sweeps, a vault and a frost slam), Arc Sweep (sweep, roll, a full whirl), Thunderhead (lash, sweep, lightning all round, 14 mana). **Warding Sigil** (35 mana): a circle at your feet for 6 s; enemies in it move at half speed and their shots break on it. Evolution **Storm Sigil**: lightning onto an enemy inside every 0.6 s.
  - `scripts/weapons.mjs` checks every weapon's moves and combos, the specials and evolutions, crafting, equipping, the wardrobe slot and the tree's old evolutions.
- The old run of three dungeons and Varek (below) still builds with `?dungeon` in the address (the older test scripts use it), and the 11 rooms in a row with `?chain`.

## Run structure (the old dungeons: `?dungeon`)
- **Three dungeons, then Varek** (`src/dungeon.js`): The Ashen Warrens (camp, ruins and bridge chambers), The Hollow Deep (village, crypt, ruins), The Drowned Halls (bog, gate, crypt). Each ends in one of the three mini bosses (shuffled per run); after the third comes the Warden's Gate and **Varek** in the Throne of Ash.
- **A dungeon:** 22-25 chambers on a 7 × 7 grid of 28 m cells, joined by corridors: a random tree with a few loops, so there are dead ends, circuits, small junctions and side rooms. Chambers vary in size and shape (wings, bays, round bog chambers, long crypt halls, platforms over the chasm with plank bridges) and are dressed by theme (palisades, tents, pillars, houses, coffins, pools, stone blocks) with the props kept off the paths between doorways, so every chamber can be crossed; a flood fill puts enemies only where you can reach them. About 55-70 enemies (15% / 30% more in a party). You start at a lit bonfire in the middle. Everything comes from the run's seed, so a party all gets the same map. `scripts/dungeon.mjs` builds many seeds and checks everything is reachable; `scripts/dungeon-look.mjs` takes screenshots.
- **The sealed door:** the mini boss's arena is off one edge of the map, somewhere you have to find, behind a door that shows **SEALED 0/3**. Three broken seals open it and nothing else matters: every device checks it every frame, and in a party the host repeats which seals are broken every 2 s so nobody's door stays shut. Five seal shrines (a glowing crystal and a beam of light you can see over the walls) are spread far apart; break **three** to open the door (`src/seals.js`). Each is guarded:
  - **Elite pack:** an elite and three others (four with three players). Elites are bigger, glow in their trait's colour, have 2.6× health and hit 25% harder, with one trait: **Frenzied** (faster attacks and movement, shorter pauses), **Armoured** (takes a third less damage, hard to stagger) or **Blazing** (fire on every hit and a heat aura that burns anyone within 2.5 m). Kill the pack and the seal breaks.
  - **Hold the circle:** stand in the ring round the shrine for 30 s. Time only runs with someone inside (it slips back slowly when nobody is) and waves of 2 (3 with two players, 4 with three) come in from the edges every 7 s. When the seal breaks, every enemy still in the chamber fades away (no gold or hearts from them; they count toward the full clear).
  - **Cursed altar:** three cursed effigies stand round the shrine. Come near and enemies pour out of the altar (2 every 5 s, up to 8; 9 with two players, 10 with three) until all three effigies are broken.
- **Exploring pays:** every enemy you kill is gold, so clearing more of a dungeon earns more; killing every enemy in a dungeon (the map shows % cleared; enemies called in by seals don't count) gives 5 emeralds.
- **The map** (`src/minimap.js`): a corner map that fills in as you and your party explore; **M** (or a tap on it) opens the whole map. Unbroken seals always glow on it, even in unexplored dark; the boss door and arena show once seen. Under it: the dungeon's name, seals broken and % cleared.
- **After the mini boss:** a stair down opens in the arena. Step on it and press interact to go on to the next dungeon (in a party it takes everyone). You keep your health, flasks and power-ups.
- **Enemies in a dungeon** chase you into the chambers next to theirs, but no further. Regular enemies get stronger each dungeon (tier = dungeon number).
- **Night Raid:** unlocks for the whole account once you beat the second mini boss of a run; then a **Night Raid** toggle sits under the character cards (in a party, the host's toggle in the lobby sets it for everyone). The whole run is played at night: near darkness under a faint cold moonlight, each player lights a cone about 5 m ahead (plus a faint glow round them) and teammates' cones light things for you too; torches, braziers and bonfires give only a faint glow (30% as bright, 60% the reach of the day), and enemies outside the light are dark shapes with glowing eyes. The corner map fills in a smaller circle round you. Enemies have +40% health, +30% damage, +20% move speed, 30% shorter pauses between attacks and notice you from 30% further; enemies waiting their turn step in every 5 s instead of every 10-15. Mini bosses and Varek get +40% health and +30% damage. Half of all enemy gold drops are doubled, and boss drops are doubled. `scripts/night.mjs` checks it.
- The old run of 11 rooms in a row still builds for the older test scripts (`?chain` in the address).
- **Chamber themes** (from the old rooms):
  - The Broken Road: winding ruins.
  - The Ashen Camp: palisade war camp.
  - The Hanging Span: narrow bridge over a chasm.
  - The Burned Hamlet: houses to path around, plus a brute.
  - The Ossuary Hall: pillared crypt with blue torches.
  - The Sunken Mire: bog pools that slow you.
  - The Warden's Gate: brute courtyard plus archers past the gate.
- **Mini bosses:**
  - **Brother Hask** (Executioner's Pit): axe; flurry, delayed chop, charge, hop slam.
  - **Sister Vael** (Moonlit Cloister): a moon-masked assassin with a swinging silver braid, a split cloak and two crescent daggers; sidesteps your swings, lunges and dives.
  - **Gorrath** (Slag Forge): giant maul; slams, sweeps, rear stomp, thrown boulders, and a leaping maul drop (a red ring marks the landing) that splashes out a ring of burning slag and swings into a sweep. Still the slowest of the three, but 25% quicker to attack than he was, with shorter pauses and a faster walk. `scripts/gorrath.mjs` checks it.
- **After each mini boss:** pick 1 of 3 random power-ups, or **Rest** (full HP and flasks refilled). Power-ups stack:
  - +25 max HP
  - +15 max stamina
  - +15% damage
  - +15% roll distance
  - +10% attack speed
  - Bleed, Poison, Frost or Fire Infusion: each hit adds 15% of its physical damage as that element (+8% for each extra)
- **Damage types** (`src/damage.js`): physical, fire, bleed, poison and frost. A hit's total comes off the health bar; only elemental damage builds status (bleed damage builds bleed, and so on). Every character's weapon is physical; the skill tree and Infusions add the rest.
- **Status effects** (Elden Ring-style buildup): each point of elemental damage fills that status's meter (×1.75 for your melee, ×0.75 for spells, ×4 for enemy hits on you). When it's full the effect procs and the meter resets. After its first proc on an enemy or a player, that status needs twice the buildup. **On enemies** one hit fills at most 15% of the meter (even a maxed bleed build needs 7 hits on a swordsman, then 14 for the next proc), and each 1% of an enemy's resistance cuts buildup by 3% (at most 90%). `scripts/buildup.mjs` counts the hits.
  - On enemies: bleed is a burst of 15% max HP + 45; frost 10% + 25 and slowed for 6 seconds; poison 20% + 60 over 10 seconds; fire 25% + 50 over 3.5 seconds. Bosses take roughly half the percentage.
  - On players: bleed a burst of 15% max health; fire 15% over 3.5 seconds; poison 15% over 10 seconds; frost 6% and slowed for 4 seconds. Your meters show as big framed icons over your health bar, glowing in their colour with the meter under each; an icon flashes every time a hit builds it and bursts white when it procs.
  - Enemies show small status icons above their health bar while a meter is filling; the icon flashes when it procs.
- **Enemy elements** (teaching you to build resistances): a regular enemy's element is **extra damage on top of its hit** (+20% with one element, +12% each with two; from the third dungeon on +30% / +18% each / +13% each with three). How many carry one rises each dungeon: **half** in the first, **80%** in the second (a third of those with two), **every enemy** in the third and on the way to Varek (45% with two, 10% with three). Elites, seal guards and enemies called in by seals included; Blazing elites always carry fire. Their weapons show it. Bosses' elements are part of their hit instead (the total stays the same): Hask hits with bleed, Vael with frost and bleed (half the buildup per hit: she hits fast), Gorrath with fire, Varek with fire once he ignites. `scripts/elements.mjs` checks the mix.
- **Armour and resistances** (skill tree): the Knight's armour cuts physical damage (up to 60%). Each 1% of an element's resistance cuts that element's damage and its buildup on you by 1%: a direct cut, up to 75% (at the cap a quarter of the damage gets through; at 60% you take 40%, 60% more than capped). The stats panel shows how much of each element you take. The full trees reach the caps; a first-section build holds only part of them.
- **Critical hits** (`critRoll` in `src/defenses.js`): any hit, yours or an enemy's, can crit for +50% damage plus the attacker's crit damage bonus (weapon hits, spells, arrows, Gorrath's rocks and fire, Varek's rings; not ripostes or finishers). Caps: 75% chance, +100% bonus (a crit at most ×2.5). **Player:** every tree has 10 *Precision* nodes (+3% crit chance near the start up to +12% at the edge, 75% in all) and 10 *Ferocity* nodes (+4% up to +16% crit damage, +100% in all), so a first-section build reaches only the inner few. **Every element in every tree:** each class's own elements stay the strongest, and the elements its tree had none of get 4 nodes each, added the same way (Knight: bleed, poison, frost; Rogue: fire, frost; Spellsword: bleed, poison): +20 then +30 flat per hit and +50% then +80% (125% at most with the cap), the bigger ones further out (tree version 15 refunded every tree once). The crit nodes were added on top of the finished trees: each sits in the middle of an existing long line (split in two), or carries a plain branch end one step further, clear of every node and line, one per line and spread round the regions; no other node moved or changed, and every evolution is still 6 links out (tree version 14 refunded every tree once). Your crits show as bigger orange numbers with a "!". **Enemies** roll crit chance and crit damage per run like their defences: archers 2-5% / +20-35%, spearmen and swordsmen 3-6% / +25-40%, brutes 4-8% / +30-45%, elites +3% / +5%; Hask 6-8% / +35-45%, Vael 8-10% / +30-40%, Gorrath 6-8% / +40-50%, Varek 10-12% / +40-50%. Each dungeon adds +1% / +2%, each new section +3% / +6% more (this first section tops out near 15% / +56%). An enemy's crit on you shows "Critical!" over your head with a harder shake. `scripts/crit.mjs` checks it all.
- **Enemy armour and resistances** (`src/defenses.js`): the same rules and caps for enemies (armour 60%, resistances 75%). Every enemy rolls its armour inside its type's range and 1-4 resistances (fire, bleed, poison, frost) inside its type's resistance range, from the run's seed so a party sees the same. At the first dungeon: archers 0-5 armour / 3-8 resistance, spearmen 2-8 / 3-10, swordsmen 5-10 / 5-10, brutes 10-15 / 8-15; elites +5. Each later dungeon adds +2 armour / +3 resistance; each new section (every 4 dungeons, for the sections to come) adds a further +6 / +9, so this first section tops out around 26 armour / 29 resistance and later ones close in on the caps. - **Weapon effects** (`src/weaponfx.js`): any element you deal at least 1.5 of on a light hit shows on your blade as pixel particles: bleed drips red, fire burns, frost radiates white cold, poison drips green. - Module code lives in `src/modules.js`, power-ups in `src/boons.js`, and status effects in `src/status.js`.

## Gold and permanent upgrades
- **Hearts:** about a third of enemies drop a red pixel heart where they fall. Walk over one while hurt and it heals 200 (20 at the old size); at full health it stays put for later. In a party the host rolls the drop and the first player to reach it takes it. A new level clears any left. `scripts/hearts.mjs` tests it.
- **Earning:** gold comes from kills and goes to your account the moment the enemy dies: 15 for a regular enemy, 30 for a brute, 45 for an elite, wherever you are (getting stronger means getting further and killing more). Mini bosses drop 150, 200, then 250, and Varek 300. Night Raid: half of all kills pay double, and bosses double. Tuned so a run buys about one skill tree node (`scripts/income.mjs`).

## Multiplayer (2-3 players)
Create a room, share the 4-letter code, and the host starts the run. The host's browser runs the enemies; each player runs their own character, so dodges are judged on that player's device. Code is in `src/mp.js`.

| | 2 players | 3 players |
|---|---|---|
| Bosses and brutes: health | +75% | +200% |
| Bosses and brutes: damage / attack speed | +20% / +10%, 30% shorter gaps | same |
| Regular enemies: health | +10% | +13% |
| Regular enemies: damage | +10% | +12% |
| All enemies: move speed | +10% | +10% |
| Extra regular enemies | +30% | +60% |

- **Gold:** every signed-in player banks the full room and boss amounts on their own account, including section bonuses.
- **Going down:** you stay down until a teammate revives you (50% health). Each revive takes 10% of max health from everyone for the rest of the run. If everyone is down, the run ends.
- **Rewards:** each player picks their own, and the run resumes when everyone standing has chosen.
- **Teammates:** their attacks play out on your screen from instant events, and their sounds play at half volume. Every ability effect (ground pound, knee impact, shroud, red slash, rune impale, cast glow and circle, explosions, heals, shield bashes, status bursts, Varek's Ember Burst and ignite) is a named effect in `src/vfx.js`: the device that performs it plays it and sends it, and every other screen plays the same thing. Jumps, the shroud vanish, reverse grips and the shield-charge shield show on teammates too. `scripts/mpfx.mjs` checks it with two devices.
- **After a run:** everyone taps Ready Up, and the host's device rolls a new run in the same room. **Lobby & Upgrades** goes back to the room's lobby to shop and change class; the host can start from there.
- **Down when a mini boss dies:** you get back up at half health (no revive penalty) and pick a reward like everyone else.
- If the host leaves, the run ends for everyone.

## Server
Runs as the user service `souls-server` on port 4100, public at `/souls` through Tailscale Funnel. Data is in `server/data/` (gitignored). The unit file is in `deploy/`. To reinstall: `cd server && npm i`.

**Pixel-art look** (`src/pixel.js`): the 3D scene renders at about 270 game pixels tall, gets 1-pixel outlines, hard 3-step lighting and a fixed palette with ordered dithering, and is scaled up by a whole number with CSS `image-rendering: pixelated`. This is also cheap on big and ultrawide screens. Screens wider than 2.4:1 get a 2.4:1 horizontal view. `scripts/look.mjs <tag>` takes review screenshots.

**Living models** (`src/models.js`, `src/kits.js`): every character is built from boxes with per-character detail. Cloth, capes, plumes, scarves and hair hang on springs that react to movement, turns and a gusting wind; armor plates rattle on footsteps and hits (the Knight's also clink); arms, torso and head get a little follow-through. The Rogue is a hooded, cloaked figure with curved daggers and an idle dagger flip; the Spellsword's coat runes glow with her mana and glyphs orbit her off hand; Varek's armor cracks glow once he ignites. All visual: hit-boxes are circles around each actor.

**Set dressing and ambient life** (`src/decor.js`): every room gets props along its walls and on its rim (corpses, bones, gravestones, skull stakes, carts, sacks, candles, lantern posts, swords stuck in the ground, blood), figures (kneeling pilgrims, chained prisoners, weeping statues) and small life that only moves near you (crows that take off as you come close, rats along the walls, swaying hanging cages, moths, fireflies, falling ash, dust). The arenas are themed: the Executioner's Pit has a hooded crowd swaying round the rim, spikes, gallows cages and blood-soaked sand; the Moonlit Cloister broken arches, moonbeams, a glowing pool, candles and praying statues with drifting petals; the Slag Forge lava channels, cauldrons pouring slag, smoking chimneys and anvils; the Throne of Ash a ruined throne, torn standards, cracked flagstones, swords in the ground and braziers that flare when Varek ignites. Props never sit in the walkable area (the few inside the walls have no collision) and are lifted onto the stepped terrain (`World.groundY`), so layouts and fights are unchanged. `scripts/rooms-look.mjs` takes screenshots of every room.

**Combat effects** (`src/pfx.js`): pixelfx effects drawn into small canvases on camera-facing sprites, on the same pixel grid as the scene: hit sparks, crits, parries, blocks, heals, death poofs, the Spellsword's charge-up, magic circle, blue bolt and churning fireball with explosion, and Varek's Ember Burst. Effects copied from the pixelfx library live in `src/pixelfx/fx/`. `scripts/models.mjs` and `scripts/effects.mjs` take review screenshots.

It runs in desktop browsers and on iPhone (landscape, touch controls). You can add it to the Home Screen for fullscreen.

## Combat notes
- **Combos** (`src/combos.js`): start with a normal attack; at a set moment in each move the weapon glints and a little red mark shows over your head, and pressing the next input within **680 ms** (the window stays open a moment after the move ends, so you can continue from standing). The first two moves keep their pace; after that the combo rolls on: the next window opens just after the move's hit connects and every move from the third on plays 1.6× as fast, so the finishing hits follow about as quickly as the opening ones (`scripts/combo-tempo.mjs` times them) continues the combo (a chime plays). Pressing outside the window does that button's normal move; doing nothing ends it. Combos branch from shared openings. Each class has three 3-move combos and one 4-move combo whose moves play 15% faster (the four evolution combos to come split the same way). Every attack costs 10% less stamina than its listed cost; combo moves cost a fixed, larger amount. Land the first two moves of a combo (a roll or bolt step counts as landed) and the rest of it costs no stamina ("STAMINA FREE" pops up). The last move is always the combo's biggest hit. A hit during a combo's first move breaks it; from the second move on you are armoured: hits still hurt, but they don't stagger, knock down or shove you, and the combo carries on. A combo staggers each enemy at most once, so nothing gets stun-locked. Combo moves turn to an enemy right behind you (after the roll steps). The skill tree screen lists them under the stats. `scripts/combos.mjs` plays each one on time, early and late.
- **Combo holds** (`src/hold.js`): a combo hit from the second move on that lands while an enemy or boss is *weak* holds it in a light stagger (its attack is cut off; no knockdown). Each combo hit keeps the hold going for 0.9 s, so it lasts until the combo ends. Weak means idle, walking, chasing, recovering, staggered, mid parryable attack, or recovering after an unparryable attack's last hit. Never weak: on its knees for a finisher, parried, being riposted, dormant, rising, Varek's roar, Vael's sidestep. Held time adds up across players (gaps under a second don't reset it; 2 s fully free does). After 4 s a regular enemy shrugs free and can't be held for 3 s; a boss or brute breaks out with a burst of energy (5 m ring, pushes everyone back, 8% of max health, rolls dodge it) and can't be held for 8 s. In multiplayer the host applies holds; teammates' combo hits are flagged. `scripts/hold.mjs` checks it.

- **Parry (Rogue):**
  - Timing: press it within the 0.23s before a parryable hit lands. A bright, pulsing white ring (with a dark edge so it shows on any ground) on the attacker shows exactly that window.
  - What can be parried: only certain light attacks (grunt slashes, spear thrust and sweep, archer bash, Varek's sweeps, Hask's chop and flurry). Projectiles, slams, overheads, leaps, charges, lunges, spins and stomps never can.
  - A missed parry costs half your max stamina, and regen is delayed 1.6s.
- **Ripostes and finishers** (a riposte on a parried enemy; a finisher on a boss knocked to its knees by a poise break): each character has its own. (These used to be called critical hits; *critical hits* are now the random crits below.) The tree's *Cruelty* nodes add riposte and finisher damage.
  - Knight, Flying Knee: 110. Leaps high and drives a knee into the enemy's head; blood sprays off it.
  - Rogue after a parry, Parry Stab: 48 (three light hits plus 3× the tree's flat damage of every type, times damage bonuses; ×2.3 on bosses, 110). Kills an archer outright; swordsmen and spearmen need to be hurt first. Flips the dagger to a reverse grip and plunges elbow first. Evolved: blinks behind the enemy in a red flash and stabs it in the back.
  - Rogue on a downed enemy, Shroud Slash: 170. Vanishes in a black shroud and reappears past the enemy through a red slash.
  - Spellsword, Rune Impale: 130. Hops up and drives the rune blade down into a burst of runes.
  - Code: `CRITS` in `src/player.js`; `scripts/crits.mjs` checks damage, timing and positions; `scripts/balance.mjs` checks stun counts, the parry cooldown, the shield charge, party stage scaling and the after-run buttons.

## Music
All music is synthesized in `src/music.js`: songs are written as 16th-note patterns and played by a 16-bit style band (pulse-wave leads, triangle bass, noise drums) over an orchestral layer (strings, choir, brass stabs, timpani, a bell) in a cathedral reverb.
- **Title (all the menus):** Varek's motif slowed to a lament at 76 BPM: a harp arpeggio, choir and bells, with a warmer major middle. It starts on the first tap or key (browsers hold sound until then), fades out when a run starts and comes back on the title, lobby or skill trees after a run.
- **Jukebox records** (song ids for `src/records.js`): `title` The Ashen Lament; `hearth` Hearthlight (G major by the fire: a lute over strings with the logs crackling, 84 BPM); `ember` Ember Waltz (a C major music box in 3/4); `pilgrim` Pilgrim's Road (a hopeful D major walking tune, lute strummed on the off beats); `moonwell` Moonwell (still and dreamy, E minor: bells, a music box and the choir at 60 BPM).
- **Exploring** (`src/ambience.js` for the world's sounds, under the Effects volume): quiet pads (`drift`, A minor, 64 BPM) under gusting wind, with sounds drifting in from either side at different distances: creaking wood, wind whistling through stone and crows in the Ashen Warrens; drips, embers and wind whistling through stone in the Hollow Deep; lapping water, frogs and insects in the Drowned Halls. **Night Raid** plays `dread` instead (a D drone, the half step above pressing in, a slow heartbeat, a low chant and a far bell) over a howling wind, with far screams, moans, whispers close by, a distant bell, dragged chains and growls. Boss fights fade the music to the boss's theme and drop the world's sounds back; they come back when the boss falls. Death, victory and the menus stop them.
- **Varek, Throne of Ash:** D minor, 132 BPM with a bell and choir intro. When he ignites in his second phase it switches on the next bar line into a faster 148 BPM version with a new melody.
- **Brother Hask, the Executioner's Pit:** an E Phrygian march at 112 BPM: war drums, a low "oh" chant and a blade clang on every fourth beat, with a faster flurry section.
- **Sister Vael, the Moonlit Cloister:** a quick A minor waltz in 3/4 at 152 BPM, plucked strings on the off beats and a break of cloister bells.
- **Gorrath, the Slag Forge:** G minor at 156 BPM, a chugging bass, hammer-on-anvil hits on the off beats and a half-time section for the maul.
- `jukebox.html` (dev server only: `npx vite --host`, then open `/jukebox.html`) plays each song. `scripts/music.mjs` renders them offline, reports peak and loudness for the whole mix and each instrument, and writes WAVs to `/tmp`.
